3. Connecting a Device

Primary path: open the Device Portal in the laptop's browser — it generates the keypair, submits the CSR, fetches the certificate once approved, and proves key ownership, all client-side.

Sessions are bound to the source IP at verification time. If a verified session is later seen from a different IP (e.g. a stolen cookie replayed elsewhere), it is automatically revoked and the device must re-verify.
